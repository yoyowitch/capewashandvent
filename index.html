Time for a cleaning?

Most homes need one a year. Busy households and rentals often need it twice.

  • Clothes need more than one cycle to dry
  • The dryer or laundry room gets unusually hot
  • You smell something burning or musty while drying
  • Lint collects around the outside vent, or the flap won't open
  • Birds are nesting near the vent hood
  • It's been over a year, or you don't know when it was last done

How a vent cleaning goes

About an hour from start to finish.

  1. Measure

    We take an airflow reading at your outside vent.

  2. Move the dryer

    We slide it out on pads so your floors stay unmarked.

  3. Look inside

    A camera checks the run for clogs, nests and crushed sections.

  4. Clean

    A rotary brush scrubs the full run while a HEPA vacuum catches the lint.

  5. Reconnect

    Everything goes back without kinking or crushing the duct.

  6. Report

    We measure again and send you the readings and photos.

Questions

How often should a dryer vent be cleaned?

Once a year for most homes. Large families, pet owners and rentals with frequent turnovers do better with a cleaning every six months.

Do you work on gas dryers?

Yes. We clean the vent without disconnecting the gas line. If we spot a problem with the gas connection, we'll tell you so a licensed gas fitter can handle it.

What if the vent is damaged?

We'll show you photos and explain what we found. If the duct needs replacing or rerouting, we'll refer you to a licensed installer.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. For rentals and second homes, we can work with your property manager or a lockbox and send the report straight to you.

Are you insured?

Yes. We'll send a certificate of insurance to you, your property manager or your condo board on request.
