Why it matters

Lint buildup in dryer vents is a leading cause of house fires, and a clogged vent makes your dryer work harder and wear out sooner.

Safer home

Removing built-up lint takes away the fuel behind dryer fires.

Faster drying

Better airflow means shorter cycles and lower energy bills.

Longer dryer life

Less heat and strain on the motor and heating element.

Proof, not promises

You'll see the airflow numbers and photos, so you know the job was done.

Signs your vent needs cleaning

Most homes need a cleaning once a year. Busy households and rentals may need it more often.

Clothes take more than one cycle to dry
The dryer or laundry room gets unusually hot
A burning or musty smell while drying
Lint around the outside vent or a flap that won't open
Birds or nests near the vent hood
It's been over a year, or you don't know when it was last cleaned

How a vent cleaning works

Most jobs take about an hour.

  1. We measure airflow at your outside vent so there's a "before" number.
  2. We carefully move the dryer out, protecting your floors.
  3. A camera checks the vent for clogs, nests and damage.
  4. We clean the full run with a rotary brush while a HEPA vacuum catches the lint.
  5. We reconnect everything without crushing or kinking the duct.
  6. We measure airflow again and send you a photo report.

Questions

How often should a dryer vent be cleaned?

Once a year for most homes. Large families, pet owners and rental properties with frequent turnovers often benefit from cleaning every six months.

Do you work on gas dryers?

Yes. We clean the vent without disconnecting the gas line. If we find a problem with a gas connection, we'll let you know so a licensed gas fitter can take care of it.

What if you find a damaged or unsafe vent?

We'll show you photos and explain what we found. If the duct needs replacing or rerouting, we'll refer you to a licensed installer.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. For rentals and second homes, we can work with your property manager or a lockbox and send the report straight to you.

Are you insured?

Yes. We're fully insured, and we're happy to send a certificate of insurance to you, your property manager or your condo board.
